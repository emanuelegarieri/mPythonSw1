Choose the numbers shown in the menus, room lists, and item lists.

Start in the Changing Room: collect the Work Clothes and a Clean Towel.
Enter the Kitchen, wash your hands, and collect every ingredient and object.
Prepare the salad, clean the Kitchen, and dispose of the Bio Bag in the Garbage Room.
Return the dirty work clothes and towel to the Changing Room, then choose Finish.

Choose Save (14) to save and keep playing.
Choose Exit (13) to save before closing.
To resume, choose Continue when the game starts and enter the same save code.
